Aetherglyph: Arcane Duels Demo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026 · Publisher: Cooper Unlimited Games · Contact: rocmat21@gmail.com

The Aetherglyph demo is a free, offline-only game. It has no account, sign-in, advertising, analytics, in-app purchases, or online multiplayer.

Data and permissions

The demo does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It requests no Internet or network-state permission. It does not request access to location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, advertising identifiers, or broad device storage.

Local progress

Tutorial progress, settings, calibration, medals, discoveries, Practice vs AI results, and coaching statistics are stored only on the device. They are never uploaded to Cooper Unlimited Games.

Google Play updates

The Android demo can ask the installed Google Play Store whether an update is available. This uses Google Play's native update service and does not give the demo general Internet access.

Deleting data

Open Settings → Delete my data to erase local progress and settings. Uninstalling the demo also removes its local data. There is no remote account or server-side demo data to delete.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be posted at this URL with an updated date.
