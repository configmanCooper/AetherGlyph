Account & Data Deletion

Aetherglyph: Arcane Duels · Publisher: Cooper Unlimited Games · Contact: rocmat21@gmail.com

Aetherglyph currently uses a temporary wizard-name and six-digit PIN sign-in for online testing. It does not require an email address or Google account. The PIN is stored by the server only as a one-way derived hash.

1. Delete everything on your device

In the app, open Settings → Delete my data. This immediately erases from this device:

  • your local online session and legacy anonymous identifier,
  • your locally remembered wizard name,
  • your saved settings and configured service URL,
  • any saved reconnect token for an in-progress duel,
  • your solo progress: tutorial & campaign completion, gesture calibration, mastery medals, secret-spell clues and discoveries, per-spell guide stages, Practice vs AI settings and difficulty results, and local coaching statistics (stored under aeg.solo.v1).

Solo tutorial progress never leaves your device — it is stored only in local storage and is never uploaded. Uninstalling the app also removes all of this local data.

2. Delete your server-side ranking record

Ranked online play stores a Glyph total, world-rank statistics, wizard name, hashed PIN, temporary sessions, and match results. To request deletion of that record:

  1. Note your wizard name before deleting local data.
  2. Email rocmat21@gmail.com with the subject “Aetherglyph data deletion” and include that wizard name.
  3. The associated ranking/result record will be deleted within 30 days. Short-lived connection and security logs expire automatically within 30 days.

Please capture the wizard name before wiping local data.

What is retained

No email, phone number, or verified personal identity is required. We keep no advertising or analytics profiles.
